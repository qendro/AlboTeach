## Evidence and judgment

Spoken forms vary by family and region. These are researched AI judgments from family feedback, not a claim that a native reviewer approved the cards. Source fields and usage notes record changed senses and alternatives. Stable concepts and surviving card IDs remain unchanged.

- [Valdete Idrizi, Oral History Kosovo](https://oralhistorykosovo.org/sq/valdete-idrizi/): direct speech supports kerr and me ndëgju.
- [Bajram Kafu Kinolli, Oral History Kosovo](https://oralhistorykosovo.org/sq/bajram-kafu-kinolli/): direct speech supports me knu in a musical context.
- [Teuta Bekteshi, Oral History Kosovo](https://oralhistorykosovo.org/sq/teuta-bekteshi/): reflexive çu in the sense of getting up; the flashcard infinitive is an editorial choice based on this usage.
- [University of Shkodra, Orjeta Dushi dissertation](https://2023.unishk.edu.al/fileadmin/user_upload/Doktoraturat/Orjeta_Dushi.pdf): Gheg gërshanë; this establishes a Gheg form, not a unique Prishtina pronunciation.
- [GjirafaMall cap listing](https://gjirafamall.com/kapuq-xcross-multi-r-l-xl): Kosovo retail usage of kapuq for a billed cap. This supports informal written usage rather than establishing a standard spelling.
- [Zahrije Podrimqaku, Oral History Kosovo](https://oralhistorykosovo.org/sq/zahrije-podrimqaku/): jakne in a clothing context; the existing xhaketë card keeps the jacket sense. Pallto remains coat.
